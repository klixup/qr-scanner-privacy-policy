Privacy Policy — QR Scanner & Barcode Scanner

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Overview

QR Scanner & Barcode Scanner is a QR and barcode scanner app. Scanning, creating QR codes and barcodes, scan history, document scanning, My QR, and Pocket Vault all work on your device. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Information we do not collect

The app has no account and no sign-in. We do not collect, store on a server, sell, or share your personal information. The app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not receive your scan history, vault items, photos, or PIN.

Information stored only on your device

Everything you create or save in the app stays on your device, in the app's local storage, until you delete it or uninstall the app:

  • Scan history, favorites, and folders.
  • My QR digital card details that you type in yourself, such as name, role, company, phone, and email.
  • Pocket Vault items and the PIN you set for the vault.
  • App settings, such as language, theme, sound, and vibration.

Deleting an item in the app removes it from local storage. Uninstalling the app removes the app's local data from your device.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device to read the code. They are not sent to us or to any server. If you choose a photo from your gallery to scan, only that photo is read on your device.

Links, search, and sharing — only when you choose

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you tap to do it:

  • If you tap a link inside a scanned code, your browser opens that link. That website's own privacy policy then applies.
  • If you tap product lookup for a barcode, the app opens a web search for that barcode in your browser.
  • If you share a QR code, barcode, scan, or backup file, it goes only to the app or contact you choose.
  • If you set a custom URL action in settings, scanned barcode data is added to the URL you set. By default this setting is empty, so nothing is sent.

Backup

Backup creates a file on your device with your scan history, vault items, and settings. The app does not upload this file to a server. It is shared or saved only if you choose Share or Save. Restore works by pasting a backup file you already have.

Internet permission

The app requests internet access only for the user-chosen actions above, such as opening a scanned link or a web search. It does not use the internet to upload your scans, vault, or photos to us.

Children

The app is a general utility app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because the app does not send us any information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with a new last-updated date above.
